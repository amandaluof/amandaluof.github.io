Feng (Amanda) Luo

I’m a CS Ph.D. student in the NLP Lab at UC Santa Barbara (UCSB), advised by Prof. Shiyu Chang and Dr. Yang Zhang (MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab). Previously, I collaborated closely with Prof. Xia Hu and Prof. Hanjie Chen. Before starting my Ph.D., I was a full-time applied scientist at Tencent AI Lab.

My research focuses on LLM agents and post-training. I’m particularly interested in how agents manage multimodal context and learn from interaction.
